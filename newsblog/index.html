2. Media Formats Explained

Choosing the right format is crucial. As highlighted by The Verge, consumers often debate between lossy and lossless formats. For video, the MPEG-4 standard is king (read more on Wikipedia's MPEG-4 page). For audiophiles, WAV and FLAC remain top choices, a trend often discussed on tech publications like Wired.

Format Media Type Compression Type Ideal Use Case
MP3 Audio Lossy Portable listening, smartphones
MP4 Video / Audio Lossy (H.264) Web streaming, Social Media
WAV Audio Lossless Studio editing, archival
